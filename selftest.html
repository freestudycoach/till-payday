Phones keep saved plans tied to the address, so the new Till Payday starts empty. It only takes a minute to bring yours along.

  1. Tap Copy my plan below.
  2. Open the new Till Payday with the button above.
  3. In the new Till Payday, open the Plan tab, tap Back up or restore my plan, paste the code, then tap Restore.

Use Till Payday from your Home Screen? Add the new one to your Home Screen first, open it from the new icon, and restore it there.
